# Zine viewer

A zine viewer shows one folded mini-zine as a booklet you can flip through.

## Language

**Sheet**:
The single portrait letter image that holds every page of an eight-page zine.
_Avoid_: Sprite, scan

**Page**:
One face of the booklet, in reading order. Page 1 is the cover. Page 8 is the back.
_Avoid_: Panel, cell, side

**Leaf**:
One flippable piece of the booklet, with a front page and a back page.
_Avoid_: Page, sheet

**Imposition**:
Which cell of the sheet is which page, and which way that cell turns so the page reads upright.
_Avoid_: Sprite map, layout, fold
